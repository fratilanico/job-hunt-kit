# Master CV source (example)

Copy to `profile/cv-source.md`. This is the long version: every role, every
outcome you can stand behind, with numbers and how you know them. Tailored CVs
(`profile/cv.yaml` and `cv/out/*.yaml`) are cut from this file, so it can be
long. Nothing here is ever sent as is.

Write facts, not adjectives. For each bullet note the evidence (a report, a
dashboard, a manager who would confirm it). If you are not sure of a number,
write "about" and the rough figure, never invent precision.

## Headline options

- Senior Communications Manager, internal and change communications
- Communications Lead, regulated industries

## Summary notes

Two or three sentences on what you do, for whom, and the result people hire you for.

## Experience

### Company A, Job Title (Month YYYY to Month YYYY)

Context: one line on the company and why you were there (size, situation).

- Outcome first: what changed, by how much, over what time. (Evidence: ...)
- Scope: team size, budget, audience size, stakeholders.
- One bullet per real achievement. Two lines maximum each.

## Skills

Group them the way a hiring manager reads them (e.g. "Channels", "Stakeholders",
"Tools"). Only list tools you could use tomorrow without a refresher.
